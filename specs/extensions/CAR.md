# The CAR method
Context, Action, Result

C.A.R. is a streamlined cousin of the STAR method, used for structuring resume bullet points and behavioral interview answers. It condenses STAR's four parts into three by merging Situation and Task into a single "Context" component. Let's break down the formula:

Context, describe the situation or problem you were facing and what you were trying to achieve or address, similar to STAR's Situation and Task combined, but told in one or two sentences rather than two separate beats.

Action, explain specifically what you did to address the challenge, including any skills or tools you applied. This is the core of your answer and should demonstrate the abilities the employer or interviewer is looking for.

Result, share the outcome of your actions, quantifying the impact wherever possible (percentages, dollar amounts, time saved). Because CAR is more compact than STAR, it works especially well for resume bullets, cover letters, and time-boxed interview settings where a concise, results-forward story lands better than a fully elaborated one; use STAR instead when a question calls for deeper context or a step-by-step breakdown of a complex situation.
